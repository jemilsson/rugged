## Full pitch (teammates, README, submission)

**RUGGED** — social deduction on Solana, at full speed

Among Us, but the ship is the Solana cluster. You and five friends are validators keeping the network alive: submitting votes in Proof of History, restarting the node in Turbine, catching up on Gossip. One of you is the **Rugger** — indistinguishable from an honest validator until your balance flatlines.

The Rugger stalks the map and rugs crewmates one by one (kill cooldown = vesting cliff, obviously). Anyone can call a governance vote; the accused gets slashed and staked out the airlock. Slash the Rugger and the crew wins. Guess wrong twice and it's "supermajority lost, cluster halted." Every player is their favorite memecoin: BONK accusing WIF of lurking near a body is the whole game.

Why it needs Solana + MagicBlock, not a Web2 server:

- **Real-time movement on an Ephemeral Rollup** — 10–50ms transactions make an on-chain arcade game actually playable.
- **VRF role assignment** — the impostor draw is provably fair; nobody, including us, can stack the deck.
- **Hidden roles on a transparent chain** — the hard problem. Commit-reveal today, TEE-backed private rollups as the production design. The pitch in one line: *not even the chain knows who the Rugger is until the slash.*
- **Atomic settlement** — game over, stakes settle to base layer in one commit.

Built for Solana Blitz V7 (collaboration track). We have the design, the name, and the scoped MVP (4–6 players, one map, meetings and voting, full win loop).
